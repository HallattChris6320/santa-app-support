Privacy policy

This privacy policy is for the Santa: North Pole Call iPhone app.

Who we are

Santa: North Pole Call is provided by Christopher Hallatt, a sole trader in the United Kingdom, who is not VAT registered. Contact: Hallatt@me.com.

What this app does

A grown-up can type a child’s first name, and optionally a second child’s first name, and play a pre-recorded Father Christmas message on this iPhone. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics.

What is stored

The first names, and a note of whether the free taster call has been used, are stored only on this device (in the app’s UserDefaults). They are not sent to us, or to any other service. If you delete the app, that information is removed with it. You can also change or clear a name in Grown-up settings.

Purchases

If you buy Full Christmas, the purchase is handled by Apple. We do not receive your card details. Apple may keep purchase records under its own privacy policy.

Children

The app is intended for a parent or other grown-up to use with a child. We do not collect a child’s name from the device, and we do not knowingly send children’s data off the device.

Your rights

For questions about this policy, or about information stored on a device you control, contact Hallatt@me.com.
