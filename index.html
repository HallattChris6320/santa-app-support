Santa: North Pole Call is a family entertainment app for iPhone. A grown-up can type a child’s first name — for example, Poppy — and play a pre-recorded Father Christmas message, with a painted North Pole opening scene.

It is not a live call, and it is not an official service of any other company or brand.

How do I use the app?

A grown-up check is shown before settings, purchases and the legal pages. Type a child’s first name, and optionally a second child’s first name, then play the message. The child’s name is shown as a caption on the screen. The film does not promise any particular present.

Is there a free taster?

Yes. The app includes one free taster call, so you can try it before you buy.

What does the unlock cost?

Full Christmas is a one-off unlock for £2.99 (or the equivalent in your local currency). It is not a subscription. The purchase is handled by Apple, and the price shown in the App Store or in the app is the price you pay.

How do I restore a purchase?

If you have already bought Full Christmas and you reinstall the app, or you use it on another iPhone signed in with the same Apple ID, use Restore Purchases in Grown-up settings. Apple checks the purchase and unlocks the app again. You will not be charged twice for the same Apple ID.

Where are names stored?

First names stay on this iPhone only. They are not sent to us, or to any other service. If you delete the app, that information is removed with it. You can also change or clear a name in Grown-up settings.

Do I need an account? Are there ads or tracking?

No. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
